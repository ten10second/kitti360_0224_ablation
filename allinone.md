## Summary of all searched results

### 1. Overview

Three complementary queries over 2020–2026 returned 86 raw API records (including duplicates) and 78 deduplicated query hits; five primary-source augmentations and three model-recall references brought the audit pool to 86 unique papers. The corpus spans satellite-ground synthesis, georeferenced feed-forward reconstruction, shared scene latents, sparse-view NVS, and many query false positives.

### 2. Trends

The field moved from 2020–2021 image/video translation with explicit projection, to 2023–2024 density/3D scene representations, and by 2025–2026 to geometry-foundation-model and feed-forward Gaussian systems. Recent work increasingly treats satellite imagery as a geometric map rather than a texture-only condition.

### 3. Key themes

1. Satellite-to-ground image/video synthesis: CrossViewDiff, Geometry-Guided Panorama Synthesis, Sat2Vid.
2. Satellite-conditioned 3D geometry/scene generation: Sat2Density, Sat2Scene, Sat3DGen.
3. Joint satellite-ground feed-forward reconstruction: Cross-View Splatter, AerialMegaDepth.
4. Shared cross-modal representations: Geo2, CrossModalityDiffusion, SG-BEV.
5. Generic sparse-view scene latents/NVS: SRT, LVSM, pixelNeRF, UniForward.
